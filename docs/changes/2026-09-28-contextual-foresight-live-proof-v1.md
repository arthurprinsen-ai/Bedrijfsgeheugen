## Permanent portal contract

Foresight must remain visible where it changes a decision:
- executive overview;
- bedrijfssituatie, cijfers and market context;
- businesscase, financing, due diligence and exit;
- roadmap and active actions;
- outcomes, learning, Brain and Trust surfaces.

The visual layer must keep forecast uncertainty, evidence state and what-if assumptions explicit. Insufficient evidence must remain visible instead of being replaced by illustrative forecasts.
