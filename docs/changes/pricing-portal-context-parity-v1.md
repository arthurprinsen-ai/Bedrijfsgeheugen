# Pricing ↔ Portal context parity v1

The pricing page now follows the same conceptual model as Portal V2 and Powerhouse.

- Company phase is separate from subscription tier.
- Pricing exposes the same nine primary business phases, ten strategic events and seven entrepreneur goals as the canonical business-context engine.
- Control, Scale and Enterprise remain operating envelopes: scale, freshness, automation, governance and advisory.
- Control forecasting/scenario capability is no longer implied to be absent.
- Scale explicitly includes the action/approval audit trail from the entitlement model.
- Enterprise copy reflects multi-organisation/SSO/governance rather than implying audit is Enterprise-only.
- Annual pricing now propagates through /afsluiten into Stripe checkout as a yearly recurring interval at ten monthly fees, matching the displayed two-month advantage.

Truth rule: never show a billing cadence or product capability on /prijzen that the portal entitlement/runtime cannot enforce.
