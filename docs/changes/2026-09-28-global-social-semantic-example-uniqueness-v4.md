# Global social semantic example uniqueness v4

On 2026-09-28 a personal LinkedIn post reused the concrete “printer” story family after an earlier printer post. The failure was semantic: changing the wording did not make the content new.

The canonical rule is now stricter. Every social candidate must be new at the underlying subject/example/story-family level across dates and channels. Text hashes, shingles, source fingerprints and content IDs remain useful, but none of them can authorize reuse of the same concrete anecdote.

If a candidate overlaps a consumed story family, it is discarded and another verified source/topic is selected. A provider create consumes the story immediately; a later readback limitation cannot authorize a replacement. User-reported duplicates are negative evidence and retire that story family. The personal LinkedIn printer family is retired.
