# Human Sales Intelligence Loop v1 — 5 October 2026

Powerhouse now treats copy strategy as part of the commercial decision, not as a final formatting step.

The existing relationship/intent/NBA layers remain authoritative for who, why, when and channel. A canonical sales-play layer now adds how to approach the prospect: value-first, SPIN, Challenger, trigger outreach, follow-up/new-angle, graceful close, commitment close, objection response or value-adding public comment.

The Human Sales Composer requires evidence-backed context, applies Bedrijfsgeheugen's human voice and ethical persuasion constraints, writes a channel-bounded draft and stores a quality result. Gmail and SalesRobot are fail-closed unless the exact action has quality_passed=true.

No second CRM, action queue or attribution system was introduced. Existing sales actions, outcomes and strategy-performance learning are reused.
