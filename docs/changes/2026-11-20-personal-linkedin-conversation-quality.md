## What current public research actually supports
- LinkedIn official 12 March 2026: useful authentic professional insight, meaningful debate and real perspectives are preferred; engagement pods, automated comments, generic clickbait, low-value recycled posts and comment-to-agree prompts are discouraged: https://news.linkedin.com/2026/ImprovingTheFeed
- LinkedIn official content recommendations: genuine professional feedback questions can be fine but mechanically asking for likes, shares, comments, follows, tags or reactions solely to get distribution is low-quality: https://www.linkedin.com/blog/content-recommendations-avoid
- Metricool 14 April 2026, 673,658 posts, 63,108 accounts: question-containing posts had **77% more comments** in aggregate. This is OBSERVATIONAL, not an independent estimate of the causal effect on any specific author: https://metricool.com/linkedin-trends-study/
- Flypost study reported 130,000 posts from 2,500 B2B creators and within-creator associations: image-containing posts +33% reactions, first-person openings +20%, question endings −10% reactions, question openings −15%. These are **reactions, not comment quality**, and estimates may reflect selection effects: https://www.flypost.io/learn/linkedin-algorithm-study-data
- Canonical internal playbook already prohibited "typ VAKANTIE" bait: https://app.notion.com/p/3a5da36aac8a813799cee34689c26a10

**Reconciliation:** There is no validated universal "always end with question" rule. Choose the response mechanism from the content and actual author truth, then compare with this author's own real provider data when available.

## Editorial route and quality requirements
1. Start with the original verified personal Arthur event/architecture choice and one relevant human/entrepreneur friction from the existing corpus; never upgrade a public problem into a first-person client story.
2. Put one nontrivial, defensible insight and real trade-off into a post that is already worth reading even if no one responds.
3. When naturally useful, leave room for a reader to add a counterexample or different implementation, or ask a narrow question based on the demonstrated decision. Never force a question ending or template length. Personal-life posts need not become business lessons.
4. Forbid mechanical comment prompts ("typ JA", "reageer met EENS"), fake controversy, like/share/follow/tag bait, irrelevant sales offers, recycled hooks or repeated endings.
5. The original `personal-first-person-story-gate.mjs` now blocks narrow explicit bait in the original author copy and final original last-mile check. It does not block a sincere, source-relevant question or override original provider/truth/identity/final-hash gates.
6. Keep personal-page content distinct from Mira and company posts. Never manufacture customer successes or pretend unmeasured product outcomes were proven.
