# Trusted Advisor assurance in Portal V2

Powerhouse now treats trust as an inspectable product property rather than a label.

Every customer-facing AI surface must distinguish what is verified, source-bound, derived, uncertain or unsupported. The portal exposes provenance, source health, freshness and evidence gaps, and the AI question route returns assurance metadata from tenant-bound server context.

The design deliberately avoids invented confidence percentages. A hypothesis is never presented as a fact, missing evidence remains visible, and an autonomous action is not considered proven until provider or runtime readback confirms the result.

The dedicated AI Trust Center centralizes source health, audit evidence, verified outcomes and the controls a customer can use to inspect how an answer or recommendation was formed.

Production status remains pending until the exact candidate passes protected delivery, merges to main and production readback confirms the live artifact.
