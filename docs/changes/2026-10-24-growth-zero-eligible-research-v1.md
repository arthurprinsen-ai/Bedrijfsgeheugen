# Daily Growth: internal evidence before commercial execution

The canonical Powerhouse growth Heartbeat ran, but had 837 open opportunities and **no** opportunities with a positive expected revenue value or qualifying buyer probability. That outcome should block outreach, not block collecting fresh evidence.

A new bounded helper is invoked within the existing growth function after its original protected commercial-action selection. It creates at most three unique-topic, internal-only source-verification tasks per Amsterdam day, based solely on fresh and provenance-matched MKB trigger signals. No recipients are contacted; no posts are published; expected value is recorded as zero only as the task's non-commercial placeholder, and the evidence explicitly labels revenue as unknown. The commercial `actions_materialized` remains separate from `internal_research_tasks_materialized`.

The existing scheduler, sales action table, canonical Brain memory and delivery guards stay authoritative. Production success must be checked after protected merge, schema migration and independent database readback; a queued internal task is not a completed customer outcome.
