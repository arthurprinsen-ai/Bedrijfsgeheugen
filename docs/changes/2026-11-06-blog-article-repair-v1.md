# Seven published blog articles repaired — 2026-11-06

## Problem
PR #5505 fixed the renderer for new articles. Seven already published articles still showed the defect live: markdown list lines inside paragraphs, whole sections inside headings, a FAQ built from those headings (questions as long as a section, lower case, with a trailing `?`), and in four of them FAQPage JSON-LD that is not valid JSON.

## Change
- In the seven articles every defective element is rewritten with the structure the renderer now produces: a heading is its first line, list lines become `ul`, text under a heading becomes its own paragraph. The visible FAQ and the FAQPage JSON-LD are regenerated by `faq_items` (explicit question headings only). Three articles have no explicit question heading and lose a fabricated FAQ; two keep two items, one keeps one.
- `config/bg-static-i18n-en.d/blog-markdown-repair-2026-11-06.json`: English for the 326 new text nodes. 276 were derived from the existing translation of the old combined block when the dash-separated parts matched in number (and numbers and length were consistent); 50 were written by hand. Existing patches are untouched.
- `faq_items` drops list markers from answers; blog card excerpts are cut at a word boundary (new articles only).
- `tests/brain-blog-articles-clean-20261106.test.mjs` scans every committed article; `tests/brain-blog-markdown-render-20261106.test.mjs` got two cases.
- Blocker register entry, learning record, ledger event, workflow step.

## Known limits
- Nested bullets (`- a` then `  - b`) are flattened to one list, as the renderer does; the hierarchy is lost.
- The text was not rewritten: sentences, owners and metrics are exactly the published words. One source phrase, "Eigentijdige eigenaar", is garbled and was translated as "Owner".
- Existing blog index cards that were cut mid-word (for example `ext…`) are not rewritten; only new cards are fixed.
- Numbered lines (`1.`, `2.`) inside a paragraph stay in one paragraph, as the renderer writes them.
- English translations of 50 text nodes are a hand translation, not provider output; 276 reuse existing translations by structure.
- I could not replay the full production build chain locally; the real cache validation and 12 existing test files ran. The Netlify deploy preview of this PR is the first full production-build proof.
