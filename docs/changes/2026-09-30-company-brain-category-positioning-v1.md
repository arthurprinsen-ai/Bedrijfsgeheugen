# Company Brain category positioning → Bedrijfsgeheugen revenue loop

**Fingerprint:** `commercial-positioning|company-brain-to-bedrijfsgeheugen|orders-loop|v1`  
**Date:** 2026-09-30

## Decision

Bedrijfsgeheugen will not rename itself to “Company Brain”. Company Brain is treated as category/search language and a distinct acquisition entry. The canonical public claim is:

**Een Company Brain is waar Bedrijfsgeheugen begint.**

The differentiator is the closed loop:

`knowledge/context → signals → impact → decision → action → observed outcome → learning`.

Customer-facing shorthand:

**Weten → Zien → Begrijpen → Doen → Leren.**

The existing brand promise remains **Uit hoofden, in je bedrijf. En dan werkend.**

## Public implementation

- New canonical category owner: `https://www.bedrijfsgeheugen.nl/company-brain`.
- Homepage production-authority projects the same narrative after historical V18 builders.
- Company Brain routes into the existing ungated Bedrijfslek self-scan, not into a new lead funnel.
- Powerhouse is described as the intelligence/execution engine under Bedrijfsgeheugen, not as a competing customer brand.
- The category page is part of the existing static NL/EN build and sitemap generation.

## Revenue and learning contract

The category is evaluated in this order:

`search/visit → engaged intent → selfscan/CTA progression → qualified lead → proposal → paid order → realized revenue → learning`.

Traffic, impressions, rank, clicks and scans remain intermediary evidence only. No revenue claim is valid without observed paid-order/revenue evidence.

## Governance

No duplicate Company-Brain intent page, no separate CRM/funnel/learning store, no unsupported competitor claims, no product rename. All future chats and agents inherit the same positioning through AGENTS.md and the canonical skills/System Map.
