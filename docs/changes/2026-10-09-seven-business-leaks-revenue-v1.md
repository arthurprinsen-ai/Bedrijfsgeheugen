Do not duplicate these capabilities, and do not count a free scan or provider send as a customer order.

## Deliverable
Add a genuinely useful 4-page **De 7 verborgen bedrijfslekken** printable PDF at `/assets/downloads/7-verborgen-bedrijfslekken.pdf` and one small, public conversion page `/7-bedrijfslekken`, with a direct public download, no personal-data gate, clear optional link to the existing selfscan and then the existing paid offer. Keep the existing `/zelfscan` unmodified to preserve NL/EN translation-cache parity; the landing page links back to the live scan. An independently downloadable PDF, not a fabricated case study, fake webinar, duplicate portal, or new generator.

The customer content covers knowledge continuity, double entry, searching, decisions without owners, neglected warm leads, unforeseen risks and AI without organizational context. Explain a **user-changeable estimated formula**, not a guaranteed ROI. Sample: 6 staff × 15 minutes per week ÷ 60 × 46 weeks × EUR 50 = EUR 3,450 of potentially recoverable annual capacity; this is not cash revenue.

## Behavioral design, never dark patterns
- **Reciprocity:** release the useful four-page download immediately, without email collection.
- **Commitment and consistency:** one voluntary seven-day measurable improvement; invite a colleague to validate the original scan when appropriate.
- **Authority:** transparent assumptions and source/evidence lineage, not credentials invented.
- **Liking / unity:** only real entrepreneur experiences and relevant peer problems.
- **Social proof:** real permissioned testimonials/cases only; otherwise abstain.
- **Scarcity:** only factual capacity/time limits, never artificial countdowns.

## Funnel and commercial governance
One canonical ladder: PDF engagement → existing selfscan → completed result → optional meeting → paid deeper scan or product checkout → observed paid order → customer value evidence → Brain learning → changed next offer. UTM references on the landing and scan link identify the offer; until independent analytics/provider readback exists, count all engagement states as UNKNOWN rather than zero or verified.

Per channel create one relevant, differentiated piece of copy with a single ask. Personal LinkedIn carries honest founder journey; company page carries a concrete business pain/solution; Instagram requires a situation, causal link and intelligible point; optional email/DM only to appropriately contacted prospects. Never restart the existing SalesRobot campaign, mass mail a harvested list, bypass OAuth, double publish or add a second executor.

**Primary optimization metric:** paid orders and attributed realized revenue; supporting indicators: qualified calls and paid scan conversion; guardrails: privacy, consent, unsubscribes, dedupe, CTA truth and source evidence. The €1m goal is a direction, not a claim of revenue. No public launch claim before protected merge and exact main production readback.
