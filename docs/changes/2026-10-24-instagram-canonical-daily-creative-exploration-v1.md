# Source-backed daily Mira content tests in the original publisher

The daily growth layer now supplies exactly one controlled copy hypothesis to the existing deterministic Mira caption, replacing the previously ineffective AI-only suggestion. The final caption can vary just one of: human opening, explicitly potential business consequence, or voluntary helpful question. All canonical problem, portal/action/metric, potential-impact disclaimer and fictional-person disclosures remain verbatim. A blog or personal LinkedIn post is not repurposed as a Mira test.

The *existing* social publisher recomputes the exact supported caption from stored current-date `editorial_exploration` and matching canonical `recommendation_id` before its provider call. Missing or forged evidence keeps the original caption; changed content without proof is rejected. No new agent, scheduler, publisher, lead/revenue store or second Instagram send.

Instagram is still blocked until the already-existing required OpenArt media asset passes all three visible-Mira frame and continuity checks; no safety threshold is relaxed. After protected delivery, next real publication plus engagement and attributable order readback are separately required.
