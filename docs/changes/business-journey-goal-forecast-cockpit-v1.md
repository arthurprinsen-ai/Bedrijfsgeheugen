# Business journey + goal forecast cockpit

Portal V2 now turns business context into a visible journey rather than only a classification.

## Frontend
- current business stage and desired business stage;
- visual journey path;
- multiple measurable goals;
- current value, target value and target date;
- progress bar and required monthly pace;
- 25/50/75/100 percent planning checkpoints;
- forecast expectation and range only when evidence is sufficient;
- summary on the main Overview dashboard.

## Forecast truth contract
A forecast requires at least three dated historical observations. With fewer observations, Powerhouse may calculate the required pace to reach the goal but must not claim a probability, expected endpoint or confidence band.

Forecasts are projected into the canonical business context so Executive Cockpit and decision context can use the same truth.

## Examples
Relevant goals include revenue growth, EBITDA margin, cash runway, reduction in manual work, enterprise value, exit readiness and residual risk.
