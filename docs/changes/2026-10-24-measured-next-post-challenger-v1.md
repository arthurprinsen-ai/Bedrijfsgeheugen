# Reversible next-post improvement from real owned performance

This change closes a missing producer-side handoff: the existing daily content generator can now consume aggregate **verified comparable** company LinkedIn post-metrics and propose one bounded creative challenger in the **same** authorized generation stage. It reuses `social_posts`, `social_metric_snapshots`, the current decision, provider publisher, `powerhouse_content_artifacts.generation_evidence` and the existing Heartbeat scheduler. No new database, agent, scheduler, permission, content publisher, API credential or delivery queue.

Before adjusting a business post's hook, question or CTA, it requires two distinct owned company posts, each at least 24 hours old with fresh observed metrics, trustworthy reach/views plus explicit comments, at least 50 observed exposure per post and at least 200 cumulative exposure. Partial/reactions-only LinkedIn data and absent denominators remain UNKNOWN. The helper records `WAIT_FOR_COMPARABLE_PROVIDER_METRICS` instead of pretending to know why a post got few likes or views.

When a sample becomes eligible, the model is instructed to change exactly one relevant creative component while keeping factual editorial/identity/CTA gates. The hypothesis and provenance are recorded with the normal generated artifact. This is not a winning A/B result, improved order attribution or guarantee. The source-to-publication-to-24/48/72-hour-metrics-to-verified-lead/paid-order feedback still requires independently mature provider data. Never change personal LinkedIn posts or impersonate customers.
