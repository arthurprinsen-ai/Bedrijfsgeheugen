# Living Business Context OS v1

Powerhouse no longer treats the entrepreneur as belonging to one static phase.

The canonical business context now contains five independent layers:
1. primary company phase;
2. strategic events that can overlap the phase;
3. entrepreneur goals;
4. business health and capability maturity;
5. evidence-backed history and likely next phases.

## Primary phases

- Start & validation
- Validation & first traction
- Growth
- Rapid growth / scaling
- Professionalisation
- Mature & stable
- Stagnation
- Loss & recovery
- Acute continuity pressure

## Strategic overlays

- funding;
- company acquisition;
- company sale / exit;
- merger;
- post-merger integration;
- succession;
- MBO / MBI;
- internationalisation;
- restructuring;
- investor / portfolio.

## Entrepreneur goals

- revenue growth;
- profit;
- cash protection;
- growth without proportional FTE growth;
- valuation uplift;
- exit readiness;
- resilience.

The model deliberately allows combinations such as:

`scale + funding + buy-side + automate + valuation`

This is projected into the executive cockpit, company decision context and Portal V2.

## Portal behaviour

The existing **Bedrijfssituatie & context** workspace now shows:
- where the business currently stands;
- simultaneous overlays;
- what Powerhouse considers relevant now;
- business health;
- capability maturity;
- relevant models;
- connected portal modules;
- source/action/outcome/risk connections;
- evidence-backed company journey history;
- likely next phases.

If the primary phase is not backed by explicit input or sufficient customer evidence, the portal shows it as unproven rather than presenting a guess as fact.

## Commercial packages

Control, Scale and Enterprise are deliberately orthogonal to business context. The package sets source count, refresh speed, automation, governance and advisory capacity. The same company can move through many business phases without changing the semantic model of the portal.
