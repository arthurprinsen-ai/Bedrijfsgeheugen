# Personal LinkedIn Daily Creative Loop v1

**Fingerprint:** `personal-linkedin-daily-creative-loop-v1`  
**Owner:** Powerhouse Brain  
**Channel:** personal LinkedIn

## Purpose

Powerhouse chooses a new personal LinkedIn subject every day without asking Arthur to invent the topic. The subject must be personally human and widely recognizable, while remaining grounded in verified personal evidence and historically novel.

## Closed loop

`SCAN -> CANDIDATES -> SEMANTIC DEDUPE/FATIGUE -> RANK -> TRUTH/PRIVACY/IDENTITY -> GENERATE -> FINAL DUPLICATE GATE -> PUBLISH -> PROVIDER ACK/READBACK -> 24h/72h/7d OUTCOME -> LEARN -> NEXT-DAY DECISION`.

## Candidate space

The scan covers broad everyday friction: messaging/group chats, apps, notifications, logins, customer service, subscriptions, traffic/parking, shopping/delivery, school/sport apps, calendars, forms, QR/tickets, household and smart devices, digital government, updates/cookies, family logistics, social awkwardness and newly discovered everyday categories.

These are examples, not a fixed topic list. Exploration is mandatory.

## Novelty and fatigue

At least three materially different candidates are considered. Each is evaluated on recognition, freshness, concreteness, human detail, dry humor, conversational value and historical distance.

Historical dedupe is semantic. Reusing the same situation, conflict, gag, concrete example, angle, moral or story family is forbidden even when the copy is rewritten. A previously successful story is not a license to repeat it.

Known consumed families currently include:
- `printer`;
- `whatsapp-group-chaos`, consumed when LinkedIn returned provider ID `urn:li:share:7510601516681064448` on 2026-09-29.

## Identity and truth

Personal LinkedIn stays personal. No Bedrijfsgeheugen bridge, consultancy lesson, AI/data business moral, lead magnet or sales CTA.

First-person events require verified personal source lineage. An observation or opinion may be written as such; the system never fabricates an Arthur event.

## Provider and learning semantics

A successful provider create with a durable external ID immediately consumes the story family. A later permission-limited 401/403 readback does not make the post safe to republish.

Where provider metrics exist, outcome learning is evaluated at 24h, 72h and 7d. The Brain learns which category, hook, opening, length, humor and discussion mechanism works, but only reuses mechanisms—not consumed stories.

## Inheritance

This rule is a required source for `BRAIN-CHAT-LEARNING-v1` and applies to all current/future chats, agents, schedulers and content workflows that can influence personal LinkedIn.
