# Brain Observability

Every material object carries `trace_id` and `correlation_id` so Evidence, Signal, Opportunity, Decision, Mission and Outcome can be reconstructed end to end. Missing downstream outcomes, broken lineage, stale current-state projections, duplicate idempotency keys, missing heartbeats and impossible `PRODUCTION_GREEN` claims are invariant violations.

Invariant violations emit operational Signals into the existing telemetry/self-healing system; they do not silently rewrite truth. Observability spans technical health, data quality, business outcome health and learning/calibration health.
