# Powerhouse autonomous growth contract v2

This document is a human-readable companion to `brain/policies/powerhouse-system-contract-v1.json` and does not create a parallel control plane.

All material Bedrijfsgeheugen work belongs to the single canonical **Bedrijfsgeheugen Powerhouse / Growth & Revenue Operating System**.

The required operating cycle is:

1. Observe evidence from internal and permitted external sources.
2. Predict or formulate a hypothesis where useful.
3. Decide the next best action using expected commercial value, fit, confidence and guardrails.
4. Execute through the existing canonical route.
5. Read back production/provider truth.
6. Measure delivery, engagement, response, opportunity, conversion, order and realized revenue outcomes at the appropriate horizon.
7. Calibrate predictions and experiments against observed outcomes.
8. Write successes, failures, holds, non-response and prevention learnings back to the canonical Powerhouse learning layer.
9. Reuse the learning in the next decision cycle.

Posts, articles, blogs, email, outbound, SEO, website changes, relationship actions and sales actions are all Powerhouse actions, not isolated tasks. They must connect to evidence, channel identity, outcomes and learning where relevant.

The commercial objective is continuous improvement in qualified demand, opportunities, orders and realized revenue. Revenue remains an optimization objective, never permission to bypass truth, consent, channel identity, legal, quality, safety, contact-pressure, dedupe or provider-readback gates.

Every new capability must be existing-state-first, reuse-first, canonically integrated and closed-loop. No parallel Brain, CRM, queue, truth store, calendar, analytics system or learning system may be created when the capability can be extended in the existing Powerhouse.
