# Powerhouse Product-led Growth

## Purpose
Present Bedrijfsgeheugen as a scalable software platform rather than a collection of unrelated consultancy services.

## Canonical product architecture
The public product narrative follows one hierarchy:

**Bedrijfsgeheugen → Powerhouse → Intelligence → Agents → Connect**

- **Powerhouse Intelligence** turns company data, knowledge, benchmarks and external signals into current, explainable understanding.
- **Powerhouse Agents** turn that understanding into bounded actions with guardrails, evidence and outcome readback.
- **Powerhouse Connect** connects AFAS, Exact, Microsoft 365 and other systems so information can flow without duplicate manual work.
- The closed loop is **Data → Context → Intelligence → Decision → Action → Evidence → Learning**.

## Product-led rules
- Lead with the product and customer outcome before implementation detail.
- A visitor must understand the platform, three product lines and first action within one viewport transition.
- Preserve a frictionless free entry point before higher-touch sales.
- Keep self-service and recurring product use visible; consultancy is the implementation/enterprise layer, not the primary mental model.
- Integrations and trust are product capabilities, not footnotes.
- Never invent customer logos, adoption counts, ROI claims, security certifications or product availability.
- Existing SEO routes remain available as demand-capture surfaces but roll up to the same Powerhouse architecture.
- NL and EN must remain semantically equivalent.
- Material changes require protected delivery, production readback and learning writeback.

## Daily entrepreneur operating routine — October 2026

**Proposition:** Dation runs the daily driving-school operations; Bedrijfsgeheugen runs the daily steering of the enterprise. Product differentiation is recurring business behavior, not module count.

- The Portal V2 executive cockpit is the **one daily starting point**: **Weten → Beslissen → Doen → Meten → Leren**.
- The top 1–3 actions and any desired drill-down must reuse ONE BRAIN's same tenant-specific evidence, score, canonical action ID, ownership, policy gates and outcome lineage.
- An action marked executed is not measured value; publication, provider acknowledgement, monetary realization and learned policy changes are separate receipt classes.
- The final two stages appear as **unproven** unless canonical verified outcome evidence and separately verified learning plus next decision are available.
- The product should be as routinely useful as an entrepreneur's bookkeeping or calendar; missing data triggers useful, prioritized next-step guidance rather than empty dashboard expansion.
- Avoid new modules, duplicate runtimes, isolated learning stores, schedulers or vanity KPI cards to meet this promise.
- Product activation success must be tested with an authenticated tenant, actual decision → action → readback → outcome → learning, with mobile and desktop readback. PR/CI success is not customer activation proof.

## Revenue-first reciprocal offer: seven hidden business leaks (9 October 2026)

The product does not optimize for content volume, vanity engagement, raw lead counts or PDF downloads. **The commercial objective is independently verified paid orders and recurring revenue**, attributed to an accepted source and fed back into ONE BRAIN. Every day reuse the existing canonical Heartbeat, commercial next-best-action, sales/CRM, attribution and sales-learning lines, **without** adding an executor or a duplicate campaign.

**Existing-state-first offer ladder (all truthful and explicit):**
1. **Reciprocity, no gate:** give the four-page `/downloads/7-verborgen-bedrijfslekken.pdf` free through `/7-bedrijfslekken`, with seven signals, a transparent example calculation and a seven-day action. Never demand an email in exchange for a promised ungated download.
2. **Consistency and self-discovery:** invite the visitor to the existing `/zelfscan`: 12 questions, immediate result, three actions and a colleague challenge. Label all modeled value as an estimate. Keep the existing 7-day mini action exercise rather than a duplicate onboarding flow.
3. **Trusted service:** offer an actual optional free 30-minute discussion through the existing `/frisse-blik` / booking path, only when someone wants personal interpretation or implementation.
4. **Qualified paid next step:** present the existing fixed-price deeper Bedrijfsgeheugen Scan or appropriate subscribed Powerhouse plan only where the observed pain, size, urgency and purchasing intent justify it; don't hide the price or pretend all leads need the same offer.
5. **Measured customer outcome:** only actual accepted order/invoice/payment and authentic value receipt are commercial realization. Provider post/DM sent, click, PDF download, questionnaire completion and a booked meeting are separate funnel stages, not revenue.

**Cialdini principles applied without manipulation:** reciprocity = useful ungated worksheet, commitment/consistency = voluntary first action and colleague challenge, authority = show calculation assumptions and source lineage, liking = the founder's actual entrepreneur experience, social proof = *only* verified customer examples or none, scarcity = *only* a real limited capacity or deadline, unity = relevant peers and the company's own shared interests. Never invent case histories, customers, statistics, FOMO or consent.

**Daily content and prospect qualification through existing single-flight executors:**
- Use verified fresh external/internal problem signals to pick one relevant obstacle and *one* low-friction invitation per channel; personal LinkedIn = founder's actual learning; company LinkedIn = a practical case/diagnosis; Instagram = a concrete entrepreneur situation with cause, consequence and plain point; email/DM = personalized relevance only, consent/compliance checked, no bulk spam or duplicate sends.
- Where a relevant existing outreach campaign is live, retain its UUID, prospect idempotency and provider receipts; do not restart sequences, regenerate sends or presume that listed followers are deliverable recipients.
- Record channel + source + campaign + content fingerprint; measure `asset_visit → pdf_download → scan_start → scan_complete → booked_call → paid_order → realized_revenue → observed_customer_value → verified_learning → changed_next_offer` with **observed, pending, unknown** distinct states. Missing telemetry is not zero conversion or proof of failure. Personal data requires appropriate purpose, lawful basis and retention.
- Evaluate two hypotheses: **A** practical leak checklist; **B** a real founder observation about one problem. Rotate only based on qualified calls, paid orders and data quality, not impressions. Preserve anti-duplication keys and the canonical campaign/one Brain.
- Never mark the commercial day FULL_GREEN merely because a published PDF/post, LinkedIn send or scan is recorded. P0 #4198 remains open until true full-chain evidence exists.
