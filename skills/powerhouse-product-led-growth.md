# Powerhouse Product-led Growth

## Purpose
Present Bedrijfsgeheugen as a scalable software platform rather than a collection of unrelated consultancy services.

## Canonical product architecture
The public product narrative follows one hierarchy:

**Bedrijfsgeheugen → Powerhouse → Intelligence → Agents → Connect**

- **Powerhouse Intelligence** turns company data, knowledge, benchmarks and external signals into current, explainable understanding.
- **Powerhouse Agents** turn that understanding into bounded actions with guardrails, evidence and outcome readback.
- **Powerhouse Connect** connects AFAS, Exact, Microsoft 365 and other systems so information can flow without duplicate manual work.
- The closed loop is **Data → Context → Intelligence → Decision → Action → Evidence → Learning**.

## Product-led rules
- Lead with the product and customer outcome before implementation detail.
- A visitor must understand the platform, three product lines and first action within one viewport transition.
- Preserve a frictionless free entry point before higher-touch sales.
- Keep self-service and recurring product use visible; consultancy is the implementation/enterprise layer, not the primary mental model.
- Integrations and trust are product capabilities, not footnotes.
- Never invent customer logos, adoption counts, ROI claims, security certifications or product availability.
- Existing SEO routes remain available as demand-capture surfaces but roll up to the same Powerhouse architecture.
- NL and EN must remain semantically equivalent.
- Material changes require protected delivery, production readback and learning writeback.

## Daily entrepreneur operating routine — October 2026

**Proposition:** Dation runs the daily driving-school operations; Bedrijfsgeheugen runs the daily steering of the enterprise. Product differentiation is recurring business behavior, not module count.

- The Portal V2 executive cockpit is the **one daily starting point**: **Weten → Beslissen → Doen → Meten → Leren**.
- The top 1–3 actions and any desired drill-down must reuse ONE BRAIN's same tenant-specific evidence, score, canonical action ID, ownership, policy gates and outcome lineage.
- An action marked executed is not measured value; publication, provider acknowledgement, monetary realization and learned policy changes are separate receipt classes.
- The final two stages appear as **unproven** unless canonical verified outcome evidence and separately verified learning plus next decision are available.
- The product should be as routinely useful as an entrepreneur's bookkeeping or calendar; missing data triggers useful, prioritized next-step guidance rather than empty dashboard expansion.
- Avoid new modules, duplicate runtimes, isolated learning stores, schedulers or vanity KPI cards to meet this promise.
- Product activation success must be tested with an authenticated tenant, actual decision → action → readback → outcome → learning, with mobile and desktop readback. PR/CI success is not customer activation proof.
